Diamond's Features Guide

Based on GIA (Gemological Institute of America)

Recognized as a leading authority in gemology and the certification of diamonds and other gemstones. GIA is best known for developing the 4Cs grading system used to assess the quality of diamonds, which includes Clarity, Cut, Color, and Carat.

Clarity

Clarity refers to the absence of inclusions or blemishes in a diamond. A diamond with higher clarity is more valuable.

Cut

The cut of a diamond affects how it interacts with light. A well-cut diamond shines more brightly.

Color

D (Colorless)

Color ranges from D (colorless) to Z (light yellow or brown). The less color, the higher the value.
